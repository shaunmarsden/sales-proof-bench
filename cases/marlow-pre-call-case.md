# Marlow Pre-Call Case

> This case is fictional. Marlow Foods and every person mentioned are invented.

## Source Notes

**Public information:**

- Marlow Foods, a mid-size food distributor, posted a job listing for a "Supply Chain Data Analyst" three weeks ago.
- A trade press article from last month quoted Marlow's Operations Director, Priya Deshmukh, saying the company was "looking at ways to speed up supplier onboarding this year."

**Supplied information:**

- A colleague met someone from Marlow's procurement team briefly at an industry event. They mentioned, in passing, that bringing on a new supplier "takes forever", with no further detail.
- No call has been booked yet. Priya has not been contacted directly.

**What was not said:**

- That supplier onboarding delays are a confirmed priority for Marlow this year, beyond the one press quote.
- That the procurement team's comment reflects Priya's own view.
- That Marlow has budget, a project, or any appetite for a vendor conversation right now.
- Priya's direct contact details or her availability.

## Task

Create:

1. a prep summary, keeping public information, supplied information and assumptions to test visibly separate;
2. a short first outreach message to Priya, anchored to something real and verifiable, not a generic "I saw you're growing" opener;
3. three questions to actually ask if a first call happens; and
4. what must not be assumed walking in.

Do not invent that Priya has confirmed a problem, that a call is booked, or that the procurement team's comment reflects a company-wide priority.
